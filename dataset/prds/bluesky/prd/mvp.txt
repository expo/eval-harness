Bluesky — Social Feed Client (MVP)

## Overview

Bluesky is a mobile social client for browsing a timeline, publishing posts,
interacting with people, and managing a personal profile. It opens as the demo
user `Alex Morgan` with a stable social graph and a seeded timeline of at least
30 posts. All interactions are stored locally and persist across cold restarts.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. No login, live social
network, moderation backend, or external API is required.

## Navigation

A bottom tab bar contains `Home`, `Search`, `Notifications`, and `Profile`.
Each tab preserves its useful state while switching. Profile and post details
use stack navigation with a labelled back action.

## Timeline

Home shows newest posts first. Each row exposes author name, handle, post text,
relative time, reply count, repost count, like count, and the current user's
like/repost state. Seeded posts include plain text, one local image post, one
quoted post, and one reply thread.

Users can like and unlike a post, repost and undo a repost, open its thread,
open the author's profile, and share its public text through the native share
sheet. Counts update exactly once and persist. Pull to refresh shows a loading
state, prepends one known seeded post the first time, and never duplicates it.
Scrolling loads the remaining seeded posts in pages without replacing earlier
rows.

## Compose And Manage Posts

A visible `New post` action presents a composer modal with a multiline field,
character count, `Cancel`, and `Post`. Post is disabled for blank content or
content over 300 characters. Cancelling preserves the timeline unchanged.

Publishing inserts one post by Alex at the top of Home and Profile, clears the
composer, and closes the modal. Alex's posts have a menu with `Edit post` and
`Delete post`. Editing changes only the selected post after valid input.
Deleting requires confirmation; cancel retains it and confirm removes it from
all views permanently.

## Search, Profiles, And Notifications

Search matches seeded people by display name or handle and posts by text using
a case-insensitive substring. Results are separated into `People` and `Posts`.
A query with no matches shows `No results`; clearing restores suggestions.

A profile shows name, handle, bio, follower/following counts, follow state, and
that person's posts. Following or unfollowing updates the state and count once.
Alex's profile also shows the locally published posts.

Notifications lists seeded likes, follows, replies, and reposts newest first.
Opening one navigates to the exact profile or post. A `Mark all read` action
clears the unread badge and persists.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Selected tabs, interaction states, counts, composer validation, unread state,
  post media descriptions, and destructive confirmations are accessible.
- Primary controls have visible labels and at least 44-by-44-point targets.
- Out of scope: authentication, federation, direct messages, video, live
  network content, block/mute/report flows, and push notifications.
