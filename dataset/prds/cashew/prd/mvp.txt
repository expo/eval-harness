Cashew — Personal Budget Manager (MVP)

## Overview

Cashew is a local-first personal finance app for recording transactions,
maintaining monthly budgets, and understanding spending. It starts with three
accounts, six categories, two budgets, and at least 24 transactions across the
current and previous two months.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Accounts, transactions,
budgets, and settings persist locally across cold restarts. There are no bank
connections, exchange-rate services, accounts, ads, or server dependencies.

## Navigation And Dashboard

A bottom tab bar contains `Home`, `Transactions`, `Budgets`, and `Settings`.
Home shows total balance, income, spending, and remaining budget for the
selected month. Previous and next month controls update every summary. Category
breakdowns include accessible names, amounts, and percentages; charts may
supplement but never replace those values.

## Transactions

Transactions are sorted newest first. Each row shows title, category, account,
date, signed amount, and income or expense type. Search matches title, category,
or account case-insensitively. Filters for month, type, category, and account
compose with AND logic and can be cleared together. Zero results show `No
transactions found`.

`Add transaction` presents a form sheet with type, amount, title, category,
account, date, and optional note. Save is disabled until amount is positive and
required fields are selected. Saving inserts exactly one transaction and
updates balances, dashboard totals, and budgets.

Tapping a row opens that transaction for editing. Saving updates only that
record. Delete requires confirmation; cancelling retains it and confirming
removes it permanently and recalculates all affected totals.

## Budgets

Budgets lists each category's monthly limit, spent amount, remaining amount,
and status. `New budget` requires a category without an existing budget and a
positive amount. Editing changes the selected budget only. Deleting a budget
requires confirmation and does not remove transactions.

Overspent and near-limit status use both text and color. Home and Budgets update
immediately when a transaction or budget changes.

## Settings And Accessibility

Settings provides currency symbol, first day of week, and appearance (`System`,
`Light`, or `Dark`). Settings apply immediately and persist.

- Monetary values, selected filters, validation, budget status, and dashboard
  summaries are exposed as accessible text and state.
- Dates and amounts use one consistent locale-aware format.
- Out of scope: bank sync, recurring automation, shared budgets, investments,
  receipt scanning, cloud backup, and financial advice.
