Duolingo — Spanish Lesson Path (MVP)

## Overview

Duolingo is a gamified language-learning app with short Spanish lessons,
immediate feedback, and persistent progress. It begins with one unit containing
six lessons, a five-heart balance, zero XP, zero gems, and no streak. Lesson
content and accepted answers are bundled and available offline.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Progress, hearts, XP,
gems, streak, and settings persist locally. There are no accounts, ads,
subscriptions, leaderboards, AI conversations, or server dependencies.

## Navigation And Learning Path

A bottom tab bar contains `Learn`, `Practice`, `Quests`, and `Profile`. Learn
shows the ordered lesson path with title, skill, completion state, score, and
lock state. Lesson 1 is available initially; completing a lesson unlocks the
next exactly once. Tapping a locked lesson explains its prerequisite.

The header always exposes current hearts, streak, gems, and XP as labelled text.

## Lesson Session

Each lesson has exactly five exercises drawn from:

- select the correct translation from three choices;
- arrange a provided word bank into a sentence using tap-to-add and tap-to-remove;
- type a short translation;
- match three Spanish words to three English words using labelled selections.

Every exercise shows progress such as `Question 2 of 5`, the prompt, answer
controls, and `Check`. Check is disabled until an answer is present. A correct
answer shows `Correct`, a short explanation, and `Continue`. An incorrect answer
shows `Not quite`, the accepted answer, and Continue, then removes exactly one
heart. Feedback must be text and state, not color or sound alone.

Leaving an unfinished lesson opens a confirmation. `Keep learning` resumes the
same question; `End session` returns to the path without XP or completion and
retains the resulting heart balance.

Finishing with at least one heart awards 10 XP, 5 gems, and one lesson
completion. Replaying an already completed lesson awards XP but does not unlock
another lesson or duplicate completion. Reaching zero hearts ends the lesson
with `Out of hearts` and offers Practice.

## Practice, Quests, And Profile

Practice contains three review questions. Completing them restores one heart,
up to the maximum of five, and awards no lesson completion. The restored heart
persists.

Quests shows three deterministic goals: complete one lesson, answer three
questions correctly, and earn 10 XP. Progress updates immediately. Claiming a
completed quest awards its listed gems once; incomplete quests have Claim
disabled with remaining progress visible.

Profile shows lessons completed, total XP, current streak, best streak, hearts,
gems, and accuracy. Appearance (`System`, `Light`, or `Dark`) is editable and
persists. A reset-progress action requires confirmation and restores the exact
initial learning state while retaining appearance.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Word-bank order, match selection, answer feedback, hearts, rewards, lock
  state, quest progress, and disabled actions are accessible semantically.
- Out of scope: speech recognition, listening exercises, handwriting, friends,
  leagues, push reminders, live streak dates, and additional courses.
