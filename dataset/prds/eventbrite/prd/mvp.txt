Eventbrite — Local Event Discovery And Tickets (MVP)

## Overview

Eventbrite is a mobile app for discovering nearby events, registering, and
keeping tickets ready for entry. It begins with at least 30 stable events across
music, food, business, arts, sports, and community categories, plus two upcoming
tickets and one past event.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Likes, follows,
registrations, tickets, and profile data persist locally. Orders are simulated;
there is no account, organizer backend, live inventory, payment, or ticket
validation service.

## Navigation And Discover

A bottom tab bar contains `Discover`, `Search`, `Tickets`, and `Profile`.
Discover shows a recommended event feed for the selected city. Each card exposes
title, date/time, venue or `Online`, category, price or `Free`, organizer, and
liked state. Scrolling progressively reveals the full catalog.

The city selector lists three stable cities and offers `Use current location`,
which requests location permission only when chosen. Denial leaves manual city
selection usable. Changing city updates the event feed and remains selected.

Search matches event title, venue, organizer, and category case-insensitively.
Date, category, price (`Free` or `Paid`), and format (`Online` or `In person`)
filters compose with AND logic. Results sort by relevance or date. No matches
show `No events found` with clear actions.

## Event Detail And Registration

Event detail shows title, date/time, location, map preview when in person,
organizer, description, accessibility information, remaining ticket types,
Like, Share, and `Get tickets`. Like persists and Share opens the native share
surface. Following the organizer updates its state and Profile list.

Get tickets presents ticket types with price, availability, and quantity.
Continue is disabled until at least one available ticket is chosen. Registration
collects attendee name and email and displays an itemized total. `Place order`
is disabled until valid; submitting creates one order and one ticket per chosen
quantity, without reducing inventory below zero.

The confirmation shows event, attendee, total, and `View tickets`. Cancelling
before Place order creates nothing.

## Tickets And Profile

Tickets groups `Upcoming` and `Past`. A ticket detail exposes event title,
attendee, date/time, venue, order number, ticket type, and a deterministic QR
visual with accessible text `Entry QR code`. It offers `Add to calendar`,
`Share`, `Download`, and `Cancel registration` for free upcoming tickets.
Cancellation requires confirmation and removes the ticket from Upcoming.

Profile lists liked events, followed organizers, and registration history.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Filter selections, inventory, price totals, form validation, ticket state,
  and QR purpose are accessible without requiring visual inspection.
- Out of scope: authentication, real payments/refunds, Wallet integration,
  organizer tools, attendee check-in, live maps, friends, and dynamic inventory.
