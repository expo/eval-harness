Expensify — Team Expense Reports (MVP)

## Overview

Expensify is a mobile expense-management app for recording receipts, organizing
expenses into reports, and moving reports through an approval workflow. It opens
in a seeded demo workspace with one submitter, one approver, three reports, and
at least twelve expenses in varied statuses.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Demo workspace changes
persist locally across cold restarts. There is no login, real payment, bank
connection, OCR service, email delivery, or server dependency.

## Navigation And Inbox

A bottom tab bar contains `Inbox`, `Expenses`, `Reports`, and `Settings`. Inbox
shows actionable report events newest first, including drafts to finish,
reports awaiting approval, and approved reports. Opening an item navigates to
the exact report or expense and marking it read updates the unread badge.

## Expenses

Expenses are sorted newest first. Rows show merchant, date, amount, category,
report, receipt state, and status. Search matches merchant, category, and report
name case-insensitively. Status, category, and date-range filters compose with
AND logic. No results show `No expenses found` and a clear action.

`New expense` presents a form sheet with merchant, amount, date, category,
description, report, and optional receipt. Save is disabled until merchant,
positive amount, date, and category are valid. Attaching a receipt requests
photo-library or camera permission contextually; denial leaves manual entry
usable. Saving creates one expense and updates the selected report totals.

Tapping an expense opens it for editing. Draft expenses can be edited or
deleted after confirmation. Submitted or approved expenses are read-only and
explain why editing is unavailable.

## Reports And Approval

Reports lists `Draft`, `Submitted`, `Approved`, and `Reimbursed` sections with
count and total for each. Users can create a uniquely named draft, add or remove
draft expenses, and inspect itemized totals. An empty report shows `No expenses
in this report` with `Add expense`.

Submitting a non-empty draft requires confirmation and changes its status to
Submitted. The Approver view exposes `Approve` and `Reject`; rejection requires
a reason and returns the report to Draft with that reason visible. Approval
changes it to Approved. `Mark reimbursed` is available only for approved
reports. Each transition occurs exactly once and persists.

## Settings, Accessibility, And Out Of Scope

Settings provides workspace currency, demo role (`Submitter` or `Approver`),
and appearance. Changing role updates available actions without changing data.

- Amounts, statuses, filters, validation, receipt presence, disabled actions,
  and report totals are accessible text and state.
- Out of scope: authentication, live collaboration, chat, OCR, corporate cards,
  bank settlement, tax rules, mileage/GPS, and actual reimbursements.
