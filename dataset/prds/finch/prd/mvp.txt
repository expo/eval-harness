Finch — Self-Care Goals And Companion (MVP)

## Overview

Finch is a self-care app in which completing small daily goals gives energy to
a virtual companion. It starts with a named companion `Pip`, five daily goals,
three completed history days, 20 energy, 35 stones, and a two-day streak.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Goals, check-ins,
currency, streak, companion progress, and settings persist locally. There are
no accounts, subscriptions, AI conversations, social features, or servers.

## Navigation And Today

A bottom tab bar contains `Today`, `Journeys`, `Insights`, and `Bag`. Today
shows Pip's current level, energy, stones, streak, and today's goals. Companion
animation may enhance the screen, but every status is also accessible text.

Each goal row shows title, optional time, journey, completion state, and energy
reward. Completing an unfinished goal marks it done, adds exactly 5 energy,
updates progress, and provides a brief labelled celebration. Undo reverses only
that completion and reward. A completed goal cannot award energy twice.

`Add goal` presents a form for title, journey, repeat choice, and optional time.
Blank titles cannot save. Saving inserts one goal. Editing changes only the
selected goal; deleting requires confirmation and does not change prior history.

## Daily Check-In And Adventure

`How are you feeling?` opens five labelled mood choices from `Very low` to
`Great` plus an optional reflection. Saving creates one check-in for today and
updates the visible state; editing the same day's check-in does not duplicate it.

When energy reaches 25, `Start adventure` becomes enabled. Starting spends 25
energy once and changes Pip to `Adventuring`. `Finish adventure` awards 10
stones and one progress point, records the result, and restores `Ready` state.
The current adventure state survives restart.

## Journeys, Insights, And Bag

Journeys groups goals into seeded journeys and shows completed/total counts.
Users can create and rename a journey and open it to see exact member goals.
Deleting an empty journey requires confirmation; a journey with goals cannot be
deleted until its goals are moved.

Insights shows accessible seven-day values for mood check-ins, goals completed,
streak, and energy earned plus a newest-first history list. Charts are optional
and cannot replace numeric text.

Bag lists four seeded cosmetic items. Owned state and price are visible.
Buying an unowned affordable item requires confirmation, subtracts stones once,
and marks it owned. Insufficient stones disables Buy with an explanation.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Goal completion, rewards, currency, selected mood, adventure state, purchase
  state, and progress are semantic rather than animation- or color-only.
- Out of scope: health claims, crisis support, friend trees, push reminders,
  cloud backup, paid content, real-time clocks, and free-form companion chat.
