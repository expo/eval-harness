Folo — Feed Reader And Inbox (MVP)

## Overview

Folo is a mobile feed reader that collects articles from followed sources into
one inbox. It begins with four followed sources and at least 40 stable articles,
including unread, read, saved, and image-bearing items. The starter library is
available immediately; users may add another RSS or Atom feed by URL.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Followed sources,
article state, and settings persist locally. Feed requests use standard `fetch`,
handle loading and failure visibly, and never require an API key or account.

## Navigation And Inbox

A bottom tab bar contains `Inbox`, `Discover`, `Saved`, and `Settings`. Inbox
shows articles newest first with source, title, excerpt, publication date,
thumbnail when available, and read/saved state.

Scrolling loads the stable starter articles in pages without replacing earlier
rows. Pull to refresh shows progress and preserves existing content if a remote
source fails. Filter chips for `All`, `Unread`, and `Read` combine with a
case-insensitive search over title, excerpt, and source. No matches show `No
articles found` and a clear action.

Opening an article marks it read and displays its title, source, date, readable
content, `Save`, `Mark unread`, `Share`, and `Open original`. Back returns to the
same inbox position. Save and read state update everywhere exactly once and
persist. Share opens the native share surface with title and URL.

## Sources And Discovery

Discover lists the followed sources plus `Add feed`. Each source shows name,
URL, unread count, and follow state. Opening a source shows only its articles.

`Add feed` presents a URL field and `Follow` action. Follow is disabled until a
valid HTTP or HTTPS URL is entered. While resolving, show `Finding feed` and
prevent duplicate submissions. A successful response creates one source and
loads its articles. An invalid document or network failure shows an actionable
inline error without clearing the URL. A URL already followed reports `Already
following` and creates no duplicate.

Users can rename a followed source locally or unfollow it after confirmation.
Cancelling keeps the source and its articles. Confirming removes only that
source and its unsaved articles; saved articles remain available in Saved.

## Saved, Settings, And Accessibility

Saved lists saved articles newest first and provides the same search, open,
unsave, and share behavior. Its empty state says `No saved articles`.

Settings provides default article text size, open-original preference, and
appearance (`System`, `Light`, or `Dark`). All settings persist.

- Article rows, loading/errors, selected filters, read/saved state, and source
  counts are exposed semantically.
- Out of scope: authentication, social reactions, AI summaries, newsletters,
  push notifications, podcast playback, and background refresh.
