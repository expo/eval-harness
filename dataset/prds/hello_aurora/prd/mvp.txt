Hello Aurora — Aurora Forecast And Sightings (MVP)

## Overview

Hello Aurora is a mobile forecast app for understanding aurora conditions,
configuring alerts, and viewing community sightings. It opens with stable
forecast snapshots for Tromsø, Reykjavík, Fairbanks, and Yellowknife plus at
least 20 community sightings. The user can select any location without being
physically present there.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Selected locations,
favorites, alert rules, sightings, and passport progress persist locally.
Forecast refresh uses standard `fetch` against public space-weather data and
retains the bundled snapshot with a visible timestamp if the request fails.

## Navigation And Forecast

A bottom tab bar contains `Forecast`, `Map`, `Moments`, and `Passport`.
Forecast shows the selected location and accessible text for aurora strength,
Kp index, Bz direction/value, cloud cover, darkness window, viewing likelihood,
and last update. A 27-day outlook lists date, predicted Kp, and likelihood.
Charts may supplement but cannot replace these values.

The location selector supports case-insensitive search of the four stable
locations, favorite toggles, and `Use current location`. Location permission is
requested only for the latter. Denial keeps manual selection fully usable.
Pull to refresh exposes loading, success timestamp, and non-destructive failure.

## Alerts

`Aurora alerts` presents saved rules and `New alert`. A rule requires location,
minimum likelihood, radius, and at least one trigger: strong aurora, substorm,
community sighting, or Bz south. Save is disabled until valid. Users can enable,
disable, edit, and delete rules; deletion requires confirmation.

Enabling the first rule requests notification permission contextually. Denial
keeps the rule saved but disabled and explains how to try again. A `Preview
alert` action shows the exact title and body that would be sent without waiting
for live conditions.

## Map, Moments, And Passport

Map shows the selected location, aurora oval, cloud layer toggle, and sighting
markers. Because the map is primarily visual, a labelled `Nearby conditions`
sheet must list the same visible locations and conditions as accessible rows.
Selecting a row or marker opens that exact place or sighting.

Moments lists sightings newest first with author, location, time, strength,
conditions, photo description, and like count. Search and filters for location
and strength compose. Liking updates once. `Report sighting` presents location,
strength, notes, and optional photo; valid submission adds one moment and one
passport point.

Passport shows sightings reported, countries observed, likes received, points,
and unlocked milestones as accessible text. Opening a milestone explains its
requirements.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Forecast values, map-equivalent conditions, selected layers, alert state,
  sightings, and passport progress never rely only on color, chart, or photo.
- Out of scope: scientific guarantees, background location, real push delivery,
  photo-quality requirements, live video, social accounts, and travel advice.
