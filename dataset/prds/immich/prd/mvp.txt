Immich — Photo Library And Albums (MVP)

## Overview

Immich is a private mobile photo library for browsing a timeline, searching
memories, and organizing photos into albums. It contains at least 36 bundled
photos with stable dates, locations, people labels, and descriptions. Album and
favorite state persists locally across cold restarts.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. It may import a photo
from the device library after contextual permission, but cloud backup, accounts,
and servers are not part of this version.

## Navigation And Timeline

A bottom tab bar contains `Photos`, `Search`, `Albums`, and `Library`. Photos is
selected initially. The Photos tab displays a scrollable three-column grid,
newest first, grouped under accessible date headings. Scrolling progressively
reveals the whole library without losing earlier items.

Tapping a thumbnail opens that exact asset in a detail screen. The detail view
shows the image, date, description, location when present, favorite state, and
actions for `Favorite`, `Add to album`, `Share`, and `Delete`. Next/previous
gestures may be offered, but labelled controls must provide the same behavior.

Favoriting updates the asset everywhere and persists. Sharing opens the native
share surface. Delete requires confirmation, with Cancel leaving every view
unchanged and Delete removing only that asset from the timeline and albums.

## Search And Albums

Search performs a case-insensitive substring match over description, location,
person labels, and year. Filter chips for `Favorites`, `Photos`, and `Imported`
compose with the query. No matches show `No photos found` with a clear action.

Albums lists seeded albums plus user-created albums. `New album` opens a form
with a required unique name and multi-select photo grid. Save is disabled until
the name is valid and at least one photo is selected. Saving creates exactly
one album. Users can open an album, rename it, add or remove photos, or delete
the album after confirmation. Deleting an album never deletes its photos.

`Add to album` from photo detail presents a sheet of albums. Selecting an album
adds the photo once, updates its item count, and dismisses the sheet.

## Library And Photo Import

Library shows accessible counts for all photos, favorites, albums, and imported
photos. `Import photo` requests photo-library permission only when selected.
Denial leaves the bundled library fully usable and explains how to try again.
Granting permission allows one asset to be selected and inserts it once at the
top of the timeline with an `Imported` label.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Every thumbnail has a stable accessible label containing its description and
  date; selection, favorite, filters, and album membership expose state.
- Counts and metadata are text, not only visual overlays.
- Out of scope: automatic backup, face recognition, video playback, map view,
  partner sharing, account administration, and remote-device synchronization.
