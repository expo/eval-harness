Lichess Puzzles — Chess Tactics Trainer (MVP)

## Overview

Lichess Puzzles is a mobile chess-training app for solving tactical positions,
reviewing mistakes, and tracking progress. It ships with a fixed set of twelve
puzzles spanning several themes and difficulty levels, so every puzzle and
solution is available offline.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Puzzle progress,
rating, streak, and settings persist locally across cold restarts. There are no
accounts, online matches, chat, engines, or server dependencies.

## Navigation And Puzzle Catalog

A bottom tab bar contains `Puzzles`, `Progress`, and `Settings`. Puzzles is
selected initially.

The Puzzles tab lists all twelve puzzles. Each row shows a unique title,
difficulty, tactical theme, completion state, and best result. Users can search
titles and themes case-insensitively and filter by `All`, `Unsolved`, or
`Completed`. Clearing search and filters restores the full difficulty-ascending
list. A zero-result view says `No puzzles found` and offers `Clear filters`.

Tapping a row opens that exact puzzle. Returning preserves the catalog query,
filter, and scroll position.

## Solving A Puzzle

The puzzle screen shows whose turn it is, the goal, the chessboard, `Hint`, and
`Reset puzzle`. Each square and piece has an accessible label such as `White
knight on f3`; the selected square and legal destinations expose their state.
Users move by tapping a piece and then a destination. Dragging may be supported
as an alternative but is not required.

For the seeded puzzle `Back-rank mate`, the exact solution is `Re8+`, followed
by `Rxe8`, then `Rxe8#`. Correct player moves remain on the board and the
opponent reply is applied automatically. An incorrect move shows `Try again`,
keeps the attempt count, and restores the position before that move.

`Hint` first highlights and names the piece to move, then names the destination
if tapped again. Using a hint marks the result as assisted. `Reset puzzle`
opens a confirmation dialog; cancelling retains the current position and
confirming restores the initial position and attempt count.

Completing a puzzle shows `Puzzle solved`, whether it was assisted, attempts,
rating change, and `Next puzzle`. A completion is recorded only once, even if
the result view is reopened.

## Progress And Settings

Progress shows accessible text for puzzles solved, current streak, accuracy,
rating, and solved counts by tactical theme. A recent-results list opens the
corresponding puzzle. Charts are optional; no value may be communicated only by
color or geometry.

Settings provides board orientation (`White` or `Black`), piece-move haptics,
and appearance (`System`, `Light`, or `Dark`). Changes apply immediately and
persist. Resetting all progress requires confirmation and returns every puzzle
to unsolved without changing settings.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Board state, move feedback, result values, and selected controls are exposed
  semantically, not only visually.
- Every tappable square is large enough to operate reliably and has a stable
  chess-coordinate label.
- Out of scope: live games, clocks, matchmaking, analysis engines, openings,
  accounts, leaderboards, and downloading new puzzles.
