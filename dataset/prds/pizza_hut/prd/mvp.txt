Pizza Hut — Pizza Ordering (MVP)

## Overview

Pizza Hut is a mobile ordering app for choosing a nearby restaurant, browsing
the menu, customizing food, and placing a pickup or delivery order. It contains
three stable restaurants, a menu of at least 24 items, current deals, and one
past order. Prices and availability are fixed and work offline.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Restaurant, cart,
rewards, and order history persist locally. Checkout creates a simulated order;
there is no account, payment processor, live restaurant system, or driver.

## Start And Navigation

On first launch the user chooses `Delivery` or `Pickup` and selects a restaurant.
They may search the seeded addresses or use `Use current location`, which asks
for location permission only then. Denial keeps address search available.

A bottom tab bar contains `Menu`, `Deals`, `Orders`, and `Account`. The header
shows the current service mode, restaurant, and cart item count.

## Menu And Customization

Menu groups items into Pizza, Sides, Wings, Desserts, and Drinks. Search matches
item name and description case-insensitively. Category and dietary filters
compose with search. No results show `No menu items found` and a clear action.

Tapping an item opens its image, name, description, base price, calories, and
customization. The `Classic Pepperoni` pizza requires size and crust, permits
sauce, cheese, and topping changes, and displays the recalculated price.
`Add to cart` is disabled until required choices are selected. Adding inserts
one configured item; identical additions remain separate cart lines.

Deals describe eligibility. Applying a valid deal updates the cart discount
once. An ineligible deal explains the missing requirement and does not alter
the cart.

## Cart And Checkout

Cart lists each configured line, quantity, price, subtotal, discount, tax,
delivery fee when applicable, and total. Users can edit customization, change
quantity, or remove a line; totals update immediately. Empty cart shows `Your
cart is empty` with `Browse menu`.

Checkout collects name, phone, service address for delivery, fulfillment time
(`ASAP` or a fixed future slot), and payment choice `Pay at restaurant`.
Required-field and phone validation is inline, and `Place order` remains
disabled until valid. Placing an order creates exactly one confirmation with a
stable order number, restaurant, item summary, total, and status `Received`,
then clears the cart.

## Orders, Rewards, And Accessibility

Orders lists current and past orders. The new order advances through `Received`,
`Preparing`, and `Ready` using a labelled `Advance demo status` action. `Order
again` restores its available items to the cart without placing an order.
Account shows a seeded rewards balance and adds points once per placed order.

- Prices, choices, deal eligibility, cart totals, validation, and order status
  are accessible without relying on color or imagery.
- Out of scope: real payment, authentication, live delivery tracking, coupons
  not included in the app, push notifications, and contacting a restaurant.
