Pocket Casts — Podcast Library And Queue (MVP)

## Overview

Pocket Casts is a mobile podcast app for discovering shows, managing a library,
and controlling an episode queue. It starts with eight shows and at least 32
stable episodes with varied release dates, durations, played states, and
download states.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Subscriptions, queue,
episode progress, downloads, and settings persist locally. Show feeds may be
refreshed with standard `fetch`; seeded episodes remain usable offline and after
refresh failures.

## Navigation And Discovery

A bottom tab bar contains `Podcasts`, `Filters`, `Discover`, and `Profile`.
Podcasts shows subscribed shows in a grid. Tapping a show opens its artwork,
title, description, subscription state, and episodes newest first.

Discover searches seeded shows by title, author, or description using a
case-insensitive substring. Results progressively render and a zero-result view
says `No podcasts found`. Opening a result shows details; `Subscribe` and
`Unsubscribe` update the library once, with unsubscribe requiring confirmation.
Pulling a show list to refresh exposes progress and retains its current episodes
if the feed fails.

## Episodes And Queue

Each episode row shows title, publication date, duration, played state,
download state, and a menu. Episode detail includes description and actions for
`Play`, `Add to Up Next`, `Download`, `Mark played`, and `Share`.

Adding to Up Next inserts an episode once. A queue sheet lists episodes in
order, supports moving one item earlier or later with labelled controls, and
removes an item after confirmation. Queue order persists. Mark played/unplayed
updates all views and the relevant Filters count.

Download changes through visible `Not downloaded`, `Downloading`, and
`Downloaded` states using bundled episode media. Cancelling a download returns
to not downloaded; deleting a download keeps the episode and progress.

## Player

Starting an episode reveals a persistent mini-player with episode title,
play/pause, progress text, and queue action. Opening it presents a full player
with elapsed and remaining time, 15-second back/forward controls, playback
speed, and Up Next. Play/pause and seek controls update accessible state and
persist the latest position. Producing audible playback is outside this MVP.

## Filters, Profile, And Accessibility

Filters provides `New releases`, `In progress`, `Downloaded`, and `Starred`.
Each opens the exact matching episodes and shows a clear empty state when none
match. Profile provides appearance and an `Auto-add new releases` toggle.

- Playback, queue position, played/download states, selected tab, and all time
  values are accessible without relying on artwork or animation.
- Out of scope: accounts, cloud sync, paid podcasts, casting, CarPlay, watch
  apps, sleep timer, real background playback, and push notifications.
