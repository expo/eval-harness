Shop — Shopping And Order Tracker (MVP)

## Overview

Shop is a personal shopping app for discovering products from followed stores,
saving items, building a cart, and tracking orders. It begins with six stores,
at least 36 products, two collections, and four orders in different states.

The app must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x. Follows, saves, cart,
collections, and orders persist locally. Catalog refresh uses standard `fetch`
with a bundled last-known catalog, while checkout and delivery updates are
simulated and require no account, email access, or payment provider.

## Navigation And Feed

A bottom tab bar contains `Home`, `Search`, `Orders`, and `You`. Home is a
relevance-ordered feed of products and store updates. Rows expose store, product
name, current price, prior price when discounted, saved state, and availability.
Scrolling loads the catalog in pages without replacing earlier products. Pull
to refresh shows progress and retains usable content on failure.

Tapping a product opens images, name, store, description, price, variants,
availability, shipping estimate, `Save`, `Add to cart`, and `Share`. Required
variants must be selected before Add to cart is enabled. Save and share update
or open their expected native states.

## Search, Stores, And Collections

Search matches product, store, and description case-insensitively. Filters for
category, price range, in stock, and on sale compose with AND logic; sort offers
relevance, lowest price, and highest price. No matches show `No products found`
with clear-search and clear-filter actions.

Store detail shows description, follow state, and products. Follow/unfollow
updates Home once and persists. You lists saved products and named collections.
Users can create, rename, and delete collections, and add or remove products
through a sheet. Deleting a collection never unsaves its products.

## Cart, Checkout, And Orders

Cart shows configured lines, quantities, subtotal, shipping, tax, and total.
Users can edit a variant, change quantity, or remove a line. Empty cart shows
`Your cart is empty` and `Keep shopping`.

Checkout collects name, shipping address, and delivery option. A fixed `Demo
payment` is shown as selected. Place order is disabled until required fields are
valid. Submitting creates exactly one order, clears the cart, and shows its
stable number and itemized total.

Orders lists `Ordered`, `Shipped`, `Out for delivery`, and `Delivered` items.
Order detail exposes a text timeline, carrier, tracking number, delivery
estimate, and items. A labelled demo action advances the new order one state at
a time. `Buy again` adds available items to cart without placing an order.

## Accessibility And Out Of Scope

- Prices, variants, saved/followed state, filters, totals, validation, and order
  milestones are semantic and accessible.
- Out of scope: authentication, Shop Pay, real payments, email scanning, live
  package maps, messaging stores, returns, and personalized AI recommendations.
