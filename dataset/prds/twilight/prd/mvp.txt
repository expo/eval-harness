Twilight — Sleep Tracking Dashboard (MVP)

## Overview

Twilight is a local-first sleep tracker that helps a single user understand
their recent sleep, inspect trends, and maintain a history of sleep logs. It
combines a dashboard, an analytics view, an editable log, and a small settings
area in a native mobile experience.

The app starts with deterministic sample history so every evaluator sees the
same meaningful dashboard and range comparisons. All edits persist locally;
Twilight does not depend on HealthKit, a wearable, an account, or a server.

## Target Platforms And Persistence

- Must work on iOS and Android with Expo SDK 57.x.
- Onboarding choices, sleep logs, and settings persist locally across cold app
  restarts.
- The app has no account, sign-in screen, cloud sync, or server database.
- Layouts respect the status bar, screen cutouts, home indicator, tab bar,
  system font sizing, and the soft keyboard.

## First-Launch Onboarding

On the first launch only, show a short onboarding flow that collects:

1. a nightly sleep goal, defaulting to 8 hours;
2. a preferred bedtime, defaulting to 11:00 PM;
3. a preferred wake time, defaulting to 7:00 AM.

The user can move backward and forward without losing entered values. The final
action is labelled `Start tracking`. Completing onboarding opens the Home tab
and prevents onboarding from appearing on later cold launches. The saved goal
and schedule remain editable in Settings.

## Deterministic Seed History

When the local data store is initialized for the first time, create exactly 54
complete sleep logs at deterministic day offsets from the current local date:

- 30 nightly logs from the previous 30 complete nights;
- 12 additional logs between 31 and 90 days ago;
- 8 additional logs between 91 and 365 days ago;
- 4 additional logs more than 365 days ago.

Use varied durations between 6 hours 30 minutes and 9 hours, with stable values
for a given day offset. No seeded log may overlap another. The seed must make
the Metrics ranges visibly distinct: `30D` contains 30 nights, `90D` contains
42, `1Y` contains 50, and `All` contains 54 before the user edits anything.

Seeding happens only when the app creates its data store, not whenever the log
list is empty. If the user deletes or resets data, those records must not return
after restart.

## Main Navigation

After onboarding, a persistent bottom tab bar contains four labelled tabs:

- `Home`
- `Metrics`
- `Logs`
- `Settings`

Selecting a tab updates the selected state and shows the corresponding screen.
Switching away and back preserves meaningful screen state, including the
selected Metrics range and the Logs scroll position, until the app is closed.

## Home Dashboard

Home greets the user and presents an accessible summary of the most recent
complete sleep log:

- date;
- bedtime and wake time;
- total duration;
- whether the duration met the saved nightly goal.

It also shows:

- the current nightly goal;
- the current streak of consecutive logs that met the goal;
- the average duration for the most recent seven complete nights;
- a labelled action `Add sleep log`.

All values must be available as accessible text rather than communicated only
by color or a chart. When no logs exist, Home shows `No sleep data yet`, a short
explanation, and `Add sleep log` instead of fabricated summary values.

## Metrics

Metrics contains a range selector with `30D`, `90D`, `1Y`, and `All`. `30D` is
selected initially. Selecting a range updates the visible summaries and charts
without navigating away, and the selected range remains selected after visiting
another tab.

For the selected range, show accessible numeric summaries for:

- number of included nights, formatted as `<N> nights`;
- average sleep duration;
- shortest and longest duration;
- percentage of nights that met the saved goal.

Include a duration trend chart and a bedtime/wake-time chart. Charts may use any
clear native-compatible visual treatment, but their title, selected range, and
numeric summary must be represented in the accessibility tree. Exact chart
geometry, animation, and pixel matching are not required.

When no logs fall within the selected range, show `No sleep data for this
range` and an `Add sleep log` action instead of empty chart axes.

## Sleep Logs

The Logs tab lists sleep records newest first. Each row exposes:

- its full calendar date;
- bedtime and wake time;
- total duration;
- whether the nightly goal was met.

Tapping a row opens that exact record in the editor with all saved values.
Adding, editing, or deleting a record immediately updates Logs, Home, and
Metrics and remains correct after a cold restart.

The Logs header contains `Add sleep log`. When no records exist, the screen
shows `No sleep logs`, a short explanation, and the same add action.

## Add And Edit Form Sheet

`Add sleep log` and tapping an existing row present a native-style form sheet.
On platforms that support sheet detents, it initially occupies a large partial
height and can be dismissed with the native downward drag gesture. On other
platforms, use the closest native modal treatment.

The editor contains:

- a date value;
- a bedtime date and time;
- a wake date and time;
- a calculated duration that updates as values change;
- `Cancel` and `Save` actions.

The add form defaults to the previous night using the saved preferred bedtime
and wake time. The edit form is populated from the selected record and retains
that record's stable identifier.

`Save` is disabled when required values are missing, wake time is not after
bedtime, or the duration is less than 5 minutes or greater than 24 hours. An
accessible inline explanation identifies the invalid field or relationship.

Saving a valid new form inserts exactly one log in chronological order. Saving
a valid edit updates only the selected log. `Cancel` or a native drag dismissal
makes no data changes.

## Delete And Reset

Swiping a log row toward the leading edge exposes a destructive `Delete`
action. Long-pressing a row may expose the same action as an alternative.

Choosing Delete presents a confirmation modal that names the selected date and
offers `Cancel` and `Delete sleep log`. Cancel restores the unchanged list.
Confirming removes only the selected log, updates every summary, and does not
allow the record to return after restart.

Settings also contains `Reset all sleep data`. It opens a separate confirmation
that explains all sleep logs will be removed and offers `Cancel` and
`Reset sleep data`. Confirming clears the log store but retains onboarding and
preferences, then exposes the Home, Metrics, and Logs empty states. Seed data
must not return after this reset.

## Settings

Settings allows the user to edit and persist:

- nightly sleep goal;
- preferred bedtime;
- preferred wake time;
- appearance: `System`, `Light`, or `Dark`.

Appearance changes apply immediately to all visible surfaces and persist across
restart. `System` follows the device appearance. The selected option is exposed
as selected in the accessibility tree, and content remains legible in every
mode.

## Accessibility And Interaction

- Dashboard values, metric summaries, log content, validation errors, and
  empty states are readable through accessible text and roles.
- Tabs, range options, add, save, cancel, delete, reset, and appearance controls
  have visible and accessible labels.
- Selected tab, selected range, selected appearance, disabled Save, and
  destructive actions expose their state semantically.
- Primary tap targets are at least 44 by 44 points.
- Durations use human-readable hours and minutes; dates and times use the
  device locale consistently.

## Out Of Scope

- Automatic sleep detection, HealthKit, wearable, or sensor integration
- Live Activities, Dynamic Island, home-screen widgets, or watch apps
- Notifications, alarms, background tracking, or scheduled tasks
- Accounts, authentication, cloud synchronization, or social features
- AI-generated coaching or live network data
- Exact reproduction of Swift Charts, Apple Health, or another existing app
- Import, export, or sharing

## Source Adaptation

This PRD adapts Twilight from the native Swift sleep-tracking app in Rami
Maalouf's `fable-5-vs-gpt-5-6` model-comparison repository. The harness version
keeps the dashboard, range analysis, editable logs, native sheet behavior, and
theme selection while replacing device-only integrations with repeatable local
data and accessible numeric outcomes.
