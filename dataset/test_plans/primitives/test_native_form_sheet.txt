<test_plan>

<purpose>
Primitive: Navigation → Native form sheet (system presentation + partial detent + interactive dismissal) (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that a surface the PRD identifies as a platform-native form sheet presents from the bottom with the system sheet treatment, rests at its specified partial-height detent, and supports native drag-to-dismiss behavior.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine which action presents the form sheet, its title, its initial detent, and identifying content inside it.
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- FORM SHEET: the surface explicitly required to use the platform-native form-sheet presentation.
- PRESENTATION ACTION: the control that opens the form sheet.
- INITIAL DETENT: the partial height where the sheet should initially rest.
- SHEET CONTENT: its title and other identifying controls or content.

Reach the screen containing the presentation action. Do not open the sheet during setup.

If the PRD does not require a platform-native form sheet, record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates app has no native form sheet."
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_native_form_sheet_presentation
</name>

Use the PRD-specified action to present the form sheet.

Verify:
- The sheet animates into view from the bottom
- The sheet rests at the PRD-specified partial-height detent
- The underlying screen remains partially visible
- A system-style grabber is visible at the top of the sheet
- The sheet shows the PRD-specified title
- The sheet shows its PRD-specified identifying content
- The sheet uses the platform form-sheet shape and material rather than appearing as a full-screen replacement

<points>7</points>
</step>

<step>

<name>
test_native_form_sheet_drag_dismiss
</name>

Drag the grabber or sheet body downward past the dismiss threshold.

Verify:
- The sheet follows the downward gesture before dismissal
- The sheet dismisses after crossing the threshold
- The underlying screen is restored as the active surface

<points>3</points>
</step>
</steps>

<full_points>10</full_points>

</test_plan>
