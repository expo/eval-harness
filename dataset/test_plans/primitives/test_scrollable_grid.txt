<test_plan>

<purpose>
Primitive: Surface → Native scrollable grid (vertical movement + inset-aware navigation chrome) (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that a collection the PRD describes as a native vertical scroll view can reach below-the-fold content while preserving its grid layout and remaining correctly inset around persistent navigation chrome.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine the scrollable collection, its column count, a card initially visible near the top, and a card that begins below the fold.
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- COLLECTION: a screen with enough seeded items to require vertical scrolling.
- GRID SHAPE: the required number of columns.
- TOP ITEM: an identifying card visible near the initial top position.
- BELOW-FOLD ITEM: an identifying card that is not initially visible and can be reached by scrolling.
- PERSISTENT CHROME: the header and tab bar around which content must remain usable.

Reach the collection at its initial top position. Note a visible top card and confirm that a known later card is not yet visible.

If the PRD has no scrollable grid or collection, record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates app has no scrollable grid."
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_grid_scrolls_to_below_fold_content
</name>

Scroll downward through the collection until a card that began below the fold is visible.

Verify:
- The below-fold card becomes visible after vertical scrolling
- The initially noted top card is no longer visible after scrolling far enough
- The collection retains the PRD-specified column count
- The fixed header remains visible
- The fixed tab bar remains visible
- Visible cards are not covered by the home indicator or made unreachable behind the tab bar

<points>6</points>
</step>

<step>

<name>
test_grid_returns_to_inset_top
</name>

Scroll back to the top of the collection.

Verify:
- The initially noted top card is visible again
- The first row is positioned below the readable header content
- The grid remains in the PRD-specified column layout

<points>3</points>
</step>
</steps>
