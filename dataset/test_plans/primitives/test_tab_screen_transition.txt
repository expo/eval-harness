<test_plan>

<purpose>
Primitive: Navigation → Tab content transition (directional horizontal slide) (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that switching between ordered primary tabs uses the PRD-specified directional horizontal slide while persistent navigation chrome remains stationary.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine two adjacent tabs, their order, their identifying content, and whether their content should slide during tab selection.
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- PREVIOUS TAB and NEXT TAB: two adjacent primary destinations with distinct identifying content.
- PERSISTENT CHROME: any header and tab bar that should remain stationary during the transition.

Reach the previous tab with its identifying content visible. Do not switch tabs during setup.

If the PRD does not specify an animated transition between tab screens, record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates tab switching has no required animated content transition."
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_forward_tab_switch_slides_content
</name>

Tap the next tab and observe the transition.

Verify:
- The content area moves with a visible horizontal slide rather than changing instantaneously
- The slide moves in the forward direction established by the PRD's tab order
- The next tab's identifying content is visible when the transition finishes
- The persistent header remains stationary during the content transition when the PRD specifies one
- The tab bar remains stationary during the content transition

<points>5</points>
</step>

<step>

<name>
test_reverse_tab_switch_reverses_slide
</name>

Tap the previous tab and observe the transition.

Verify:
- The content area moves with a visible horizontal slide
- The reverse slide moves in the opposite direction from the forward slide
- The previous tab's identifying content is visible when the transition finishes

<points>3</points>
</step>
</steps>

<full_points>8</full_points>

</test_plan>
