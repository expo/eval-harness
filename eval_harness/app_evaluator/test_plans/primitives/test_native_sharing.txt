<purpose>
Primitive: Device → Native sharing → Share sheet trigger + Cancellation handling (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that a share action opens the native OS share sheet with content, and that cancelling the share sheet returns the user to the app without error or state change.

This bundles the two Native-sharing primitives, which share one flow.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine where the app exposes a share action and what it shares.
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- SHARE AFFORDANCE: where the app exposes sharing (a share icon on a detail screen, a "Share" menu item, a share button on a record) and what content it shares (a link, a message, a record summary).

Setup for the formal test:
- Reach the screen with the share affordance (signed-in / unlocked), seeding any record needed so the share target exists (e.g. create a record, open its detail where the share button lives).
- Do NOT trigger the share during setup.

If the PRD indicates the app exposes no native share action, record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates app has no native share action."
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_share_sheet_opens_and_cancels_cleanly
</name>

Trigger the share affordance per the PRD.

Verify:
- The native OS share sheet appears (the system share UI — a sheet listing share destinations / actions such as Messages, Copy, AirDrop, etc.) rather than the action doing nothing or showing an in-app error
- (non-fatal) The share sheet reflects the content being shared where observable (a preview / title of the shared item appears in the sheet), consistent with what the PRD says is shared

Cancel / dismiss the share sheet (tap Cancel, or dismiss it) WITHOUT completing a share.

Verify:
- After cancelling, the app returns to the screen the share was triggered from, with no crash and no error dialog
- The app's state is unchanged by the cancelled share (the record / screen the share was triggered from is intact — cancelling shared nothing and mutated nothing)

<points>4</points>
</step>
</steps>
