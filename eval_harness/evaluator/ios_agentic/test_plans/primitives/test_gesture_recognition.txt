<purpose>
Primitive: Device → Gesture recognition → Swipe gesture + Long press (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that the app recognizes a non-tap gesture the PRD assigns a behavior to: a directional SWIPE fires its action when performed in the intended direction (and ideally does nothing in the wrong direction), or a LONG PRESS fires its action when the hold exceeds the threshold. Whichever gesture(s) the PRD exposes are the ones tested.

This bundles two Gesture-recognition primitives; an app typically exposes one or the other (e.g. swipe-to-delete on a row, or long-press for a context menu). Pan tracking (continuous deltas) is a separate primitive not covered here.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine which gesture(s) the app assigns a behavior to and on which element.
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- GESTURE + TARGET + EXPECTED ACTION: which gesture the app handles and what it does. Common cases: swipe-LEFT on a list row reveals / performs delete or archive; long-press on an item opens a context menu or selection mode; swipe between cards/pages.
- Choose the SIMPLEST gesture the PRD specifies that operates on an element you can create / reach.

Setup for the formal test:
- Reach the screen where the gesture applies (signed-in / unlocked), and ensure the target element exists (e.g. create a record so a swipeable row is present).
- Do NOT perform the gesture during setup.

If the PRD indicates the app assigns no behavior to any swipe or long-press gesture (interaction is tap-only), record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates app has no swipe or long-press gesture behavior."
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_gesture_in_intended_form_fires_its_action
</name>

Perform the PRD-specified gesture on its target in the INTENDED form — e.g. swipe the row in the documented direction, or hold (long-press) the element past the threshold.

Verify:
- The gesture's documented action occurs (the swipe reveals / triggers its affordance such as a delete/archive button or removes the row; the long-press opens its context menu / selection mode / preview — whatever the PRD specifies) — confirming the gesture recognizer fired
- The result is the SPECIFIC documented action, not an unrelated side effect (e.g. a swipe-to-delete revealed delete, rather than navigating into a detail as a plain tap would)
- (non-fatal) For a SWIPE: a gesture in the OPPOSITE / wrong direction (or a too-short tap where a long-press is required) does NOT fire the action — the recognizer discriminates direction / duration rather than firing on any touch. (Perform this counter-check only if it does not risk an unintended destructive action.)

[Commentary: If the gesture's action is destructive (e.g. swipe-to-delete that immediately deletes), it is acceptable to let it complete on a throwaway seeded record, or to stop at the revealed affordance without confirming, per what the PRD's gesture actually does.]

</step>
