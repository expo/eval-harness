<test_plan>

<purpose>
Primitive: Navigation → Modal presentation → Sheet detents (APP-AGNOSTIC).
Verify that a modal presented as a resizable sheet appears at its initial detent (snap height) and can be dragged to another configured detent — i.e. the sheet supports multiple snap heights rather than being a single fixed-height overlay.

This test plan is APP-AGNOSTIC. Use the supplied App PRD to determine which surface is presented as a multi-detent sheet (e.g. a form sheet, a filter sheet, a detail sheet with a small and large snap point).
</purpose>

<seeding_and_precondition>
Read the supplied App PRD to determine:

- DETENT SHEET: a surface the PRD specifies should be presented as a sheet with MORE THAN ONE allowed detent (e.g. a half-height and a full-height snap, or small/medium/large). A plain full-screen modal with a single height does NOT exercise this primitive.
- HOW TO PRESENT IT and the expected initial detent.

Setup for the formal test:
- Reach the screen from which the detent sheet is presented (signed-in / unlocked, any required records seeded).
- Do NOT present the sheet during setup — the formal step presents it and manipulates its detents.

If the PRD does NOT specify any multi-detent sheet (the app's modals are single-height, or it has no sheets), record this primitive as not-applicable and immediately call complete_step with the note "N/A: PRD indicates app has no multi-detent sheet." Note: most apps will be N/A here — multi-detent sheets are a specialized affordance.
</seeding_and_precondition>

<steps>
<step>

<name>
test_sheet_opens_at_initial_detent_and_resizes
</name>

Present the sheet via the PRD-specified action.

Verify:
- The sheet appears at its initial detent — it occupies a partial portion of the screen (per the PRD's initial snap height) with the underlying screen still partially visible behind/around it, rather than instantly covering the full screen
- (non-fatal) A drag handle / grabber is visible at the top of the sheet, indicating it is resizable

Drag the sheet's handle (swipe up on the handle / sheet top) toward a larger configured detent.

Verify:
- The sheet resizes to a different detent and SNAPS to a configured height (it grew to the larger snap point and held there) rather than ignoring the drag or dismissing — confirming more than one detent is configured
- (non-fatal) Content inside the sheet remains usable / correctly laid out at the new detent (no clipped or broken layout after resizing)

[Commentary: After the verifications, dismiss the sheet (drag down past the smallest detent or use its close affordance) so no state is left open.]

<points>4</points>
</step>
</steps>

<full_points>4</full_points>

</test_plan>
